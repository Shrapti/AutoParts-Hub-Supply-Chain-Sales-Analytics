# 🚗 AutoParts Hub – Supply Chain & Sales Analytics

## 📌 Project Overview

**AutoParts Hub – Supply Chain & Sales Analytics** is a business intelligence project developed using Microsoft Power BI to analyze automotive parts sales and supply chain operations.

The project focuses on understanding sales performance, dealer contributions, warehouse operations, order fulfillment, and delivery efficiency through interactive dashboards and visualizations.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

* Analyze overall sales performance and revenue trends.
* Evaluate dealer and product performance.
* Monitor warehouse operations and inventory distribution.
* Analyze order fulfillment and delivery performance.
* Identify delayed orders and potential supply chain bottlenecks.
* Generate actionable insights to support business decisions.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

* **Microsoft Power BI:** Dashboard development and data visualization.
* **Power Query:** Data cleaning and transformation.
* **DAX:** Calculated measures and KPIs.
* **Data Modeling:** Relationships between sales, products, dealers, warehouses, orders, and deliveries.
* **SQL / Database:** Source data management, where applicable.

## 📊 Dashboard Features

* Sales performance analysis
* Product and dealer performance
* Supply chain monitoring
* Warehouse and order analysis
* Delivery performance tracking
* Interactive reports and KPI visualizations

## 💡 Key Business Insights

The project explores important business questions, including:

* Which products and dealers contribute most to sales?
* Where are delivery delays occurring?
* How efficiently are orders being fulfilled?
* Which areas require improvements in supply chain operations?
* How can sales and operational performance be improved?
